# CARE Lab Website

Interactive figures for the National Survey of Psychology Majors project page at [texasnabors.org](https://texasnabors.org/projects/national-survey-of-psychology-majors/). The CARE Lab (CAllahan REsearch Lab) is part of Texas NABORS at The University of Texas at Dallas.

## Files

- `NSPM_Map_Universities.html` shows the undergraduate institutions attended by survey participants.
- `NSPM_Interactive_WorkforceDecline.html` shows the share of national demand for psychologists that can be met, 2022 to 2037.
- `NSPM_Interactive_DisadvantageGap.html` shows the share of need for psychologists that is met, by state disadvantage.

Each file is self-contained and is embedded on the project page in an iframe.

## Sources

The workforce figures come from Cicero, Vasquez, Levin-Aspenson, and Schwartz (2025), based on the HRSA Health Workforce Simulation Model. The disadvantage figures come from Ruggero and Gum (2025). Source notes appear within each figure.
